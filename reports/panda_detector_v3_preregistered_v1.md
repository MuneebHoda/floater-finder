# Detector v3: training-only context experiment

This specification and implementation are committed before the experiment runs. It adds seven image-vector anomaly signals without changing v1/v2 or their negative findings. The primary new hypothesis is **robust NN5**: a component's median five-neighbor cosine distance, standardized by the median and MAD of the remaining tissue's own five-neighbor distances. A fixed 0.01 MAD floor prevents near-zero scale instability.

All seven methods are reported: NN1, NN5, robust NN5, candidate-versus-background centroid distance, nearest other-component centroid distance, NN5 using tissue at least 500 µm away, and nearest-five training-slide centroid distance. Each cohort reference slide contributes one normalized mean embedding, irrespective of tile count. Relative pairwise distances are permitted; absolute position, host/donor identity, grade labels, native annotation and synthetic truth are not detector inputs.

The preserved 72 clean training slides and 120 existing positive-dose training S1 cases are used. Existing donor indices and right-edge placement are reconstructed exactly; no WSI reading, pixel compositing, inference or new contamination draw is involved. Checks enforce preserved host features/coordinates and exact frozen top-5% benign/cancer predictions. The known 24 calibration slides receive no new scoring and the 24 final-test slides remain sealed.

Five folds are assigned deterministically from slide-ID hashes with seed 20261022, without looking at outcomes. Each outer fold fits cohort references and burden thresholds using clean fitting slides only. A candidate fitting slide is omitted from its own centroid reference. Thresholds are the most permissive strict `score > threshold` point satisfying at most 10% alerted clean fitting slides and 0.5 components per clean fitting slide. Ties are never split. Unmapped slides remain in denominators as explicit abstentions, empty exclusions are unevaluable, and no-context scores are unavailable rather than imputed. Every host's five positive doses remain together in its fold.

Stage 1 shortlists visual anomalies alone. Stage 2 reports signed cancer-margin influence and decision changes on that unchanged shortlist. The comparator applies the historical influence ≥0.01 AND veto to the same shortlist. Native annotated cancer flags and hypothetical filtering damage are measured separately; no tissue is removed or classified as foreign merely because its prediction is influential.

We report all chosen operating points, full tied-score precision/recall curves with equal total host weight, nine fold-fit quantile sensitivity/burden points, native-cancer-versus-foreign score distributions, clean alert Wilson intervals and host-cluster bootstrap recall/burden intervals (2,000 draws). No post-outcome primary-model switch is permitted. A 20% fragment-recall engineering target is retained alongside burden; an unsuccessful primary remains unsuccessful.

Limitations are predeclared. Slide IDs do not establish patient independence. Reused cancer donor slides can be in a fitting-fold cohort reference; they are not excluded by synthetic source identity, because that would leak injection truth into feature construction. The within-slide primary does not use this reference. Cohort results cannot establish donor-disjoint generalization. Right-edge embedding insertion and observed-RGB components are retained historical proxies, not genuine contaminants or pathologist-confirmed pieces. OOF results are training development, not independent validation or clinical validity.

Execution: CPU Slurm only, 2 CPUs, 8 GB, at most 20 minutes on `bfqi-delta-cpu` / `cpu-interactive`. Exact source and configuration hashes, resource usage and output artifacts are recorded after execution in a separate results commit.
