# S2 development protocol v1

This protocol is committed before any new S2 contamination outcome. It preserves the existing zero-shot CONCH v1 prompts, embeddings, classwise pooling and argmax tie convention. It uses training and calibration slides only. Calibration has been observed previously and is development evidence, not independent confirmation. All 24 final-test slides remain unscored.

The primary is **mean pooling**, chosen from the existing clean low/high results: training 35/48 (22/24 low and 13/24 high), calibration 12/16 (8/8 low and 4/8 high). Top-1% achieves 29/48 and 10/16; top-5% achieves 30/48 and 11/16. All are retained as secondary comparisons. The binary zero-shot baseline is imperfect and is not a clinically validated grade detector; no thresholds or prompts change.

Hosts are original ISUP1 Radboud slides. Primary donors are different ISUP4–5 slides, with every selected tile carrying at least 10% native GP4+5 annotation. Controls are (i) different ISUP1 GP3-positive, GP4/5-absent tiles, (ii) ISUP4–5 GP4-positive, GP5-absent tiles, and (iii) ISUP4–5 GP5-positive tiles. The latter is reported as unavailable if masks/connected capacity do not support it. Fractions come from aligned native masks, not slide-level grades. Missing or invalid masks are unavailable, never zero-positive ground truth. Conditions can reuse donors and overlap; they are not independent cohorts.

Use unique four-connected eligible donor tiles without filling sampled gaps. For each host/condition choose one donor and a fixed seeded connected order; increasing doses use nested prefixes. Prefer components that support the ceiling of 10% of the original host tile count; among those use SHA256(seed/host/condition/donor) order, otherwise the greatest capacity and report exclusions. Doses are 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5 and 10%, with ceil(percent × original host count / 100) foreign tiles. The host is unchanged and the donor is translated three grid steps past its right edge with grid-aligned y. This artificial spatial layout is not natural contamination.

Report all three poolings, original-correct denominators separately from initially-high host predictions, low→high new errors, high→low reversals, raw scores and signed high-minus-low margins, requested/realized dose and physical mask/footprint area, components/capacities, exclusions, distinct slides and exact oracle recovery. Any oracle failure stops the run. Different slides are not verified different patients. Native GP4/5 component exclusion and same-fragment visual distances in host versus original donor context are annotation-informed safety diagnostics, not a blinded foreignness detector. Empty native exclusions are unevaluable.

Uncertainty is descriptive: Wilson intervals for per-dose unique-host proportions; repeated doses, reused donors, and unknown patient identity prevent counting mixtures as independent patients. No p-values or clinical grading claims. No final-test job is prepared or authorized by this protocol. Existing outputs and v1 frozen rejection stay immutable.
