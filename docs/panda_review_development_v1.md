# Review-policy development specification v1

This specification is fixed before executing the new component-review measurements. Completed acquisition, tiling, GPU encoding, clean baseline and S1 draws are reused without replacement. No test predictions are authorized.

The primary topology proxy is raw RGB foreground (`min RGB < 220`, chroma >10) at the observed factor-16 pyramid level, bounded to four million pixels. Use eight-connected foreground pixels, with no closing, dilation or invented gap links. Assign a saved tile only if one component contains at least 80% of its observed foreground pixels and foreground covers at least 10% of its projected footprint. Unassigned tiles remain in every counterfactual. Review abstains when fewer than 90% of a case's saved tiles map unambiguously. One-component empty exclusions remain unevaluable. A separate 235/5 RGB sensitivity check reports stability; it cannot be selected using predictions. These RGB components are not pathologist-verified physical pieces.

Synthetic mixtures reuse the exact saved S1 tile indices and translations. Project the corresponding existing donor RGB exports onto the same physical overview grid and union observed foreground, then recompute connectivity. Never assign a component using the synthetic truth mask. Projection uses pixel-center sampling within the recorded tile footprint. RGB foreground area at this resolution is an approximation; bbox isolation is a conservative lower bound, not exact tissue separation.

For every evaluable component, use median tile distance to the mean of up to five nearest outside-component cosine neighbors. Influence is the signed reduction of the frozen top-5% cancer-versus-benign margin under exclusion; decision dependence is a label change. Features never use native Gleason annotations or synthetic injection truth. Those fields enter metrics and negative controls only. Numerical comparison with the stored classifier requires the same prediction and score agreement at absolute 1e-7 / relative 1e-6 tolerance.

The combined rule requires high visual inconsistency, positive cancer influence (or cancer-to-benign decision change), small observed RGB area and bbox-isolation evidence. The fixed training grid has **81** combinations: visual thresholds from 90/95/99th percentiles of unmodified training components; influence flip/0.01/0.02; maximum area 0.05/0.25/1 mm²; minimum bbox gap 0/0.1/0.2 mm. Select using training only, validate one point on calibration, and never try a runner-up after seeing calibration failure.

User-confirmed operating targets: at most **10% unmodified-slide alerts** and **0.5 alerted components per unmodified slide on average**. Additional predeclared engineering safety checks: at most 10% of annotation-positive unmodified cancer slides have an annotated-cancer component flagged, and hypothetical filtering damages at most 5% of originally correct unmodified predictions. These are development selection targets, not clinical guarantees. A useful point must additionally recover at least 25% of newly induced errors and hit at least 20% of synthetic fragments on both training and calibration. If a split has no newly induced errors, recovery is undefined and does not pass this evidence gate.

Compare the selected point with visual-only, unthresholded decision-dependence, spatial-only, and 20 component-size-matched random controls. Random seeds and doses are repeated measurements, not independent patients. Record forced size matches when no alternate component exists. Selection satisfies all burden/harm constraints before ranking fragment recall and induced-error recovery. AUROC alone does not determine selection.

Report tile and fragment recall, complete-fragment capture, unmodified-slide alerts, mask-known genuine-cancer components flagged, reviewer burden, genuine-host tiles flagged, hypothetical clean damage, and recovery among originally correct hosts that acquired a cancer error. Initial false positives are excluded from the induced-error denominator. Unknown annotations and empty counterfactuals stay unknown/unevaluable. Final source hashes, policy thresholds and statistical plan will be frozen after this development-only measurement; final execution remains a separate explicitly gated step.
